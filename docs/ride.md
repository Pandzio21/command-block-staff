# Ride the Staff

Press **G** while holding the staff to mount; press again to dismount (rebindable under
**Options → Controls**). Look where you want to go and move — the staff carries you, leaning into climbs
and dives, with a firework trail off the tip (offset, spread, count and shoot-out direction are all
tunable). In first person the trail streams out behind you instead of filling the screen.

Your head tracks around like normal F5 third-person rather than being locked to the flight pose.

Hit the ground at speed and you're thrown off — the staff pops out of your hand, and everything it was doing
stops with it: billboards, beam grip, the hold animation. There's a toggle to turn crash-landing off, and
you won't take a pile of fall damage after a flight either. Other players see you riding.

**GeckoLib is optional.** It only supplies the animated model — the flight itself is plain movement and
works fine without it. See [Installation](installation.md) for the 1.21.1 caveat.
