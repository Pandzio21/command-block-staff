# Projectile

Fires from the staff tip at whatever you're aiming at. **V** opens the launcher, **Left Alt** cycles modes.

- **Arrow Cannon** — hold right-click and spam whatever you've picked: arrows, fireballs, TNT, food, or
  something of your own. Arrows home in on the mob you're aiming at.
- **Advanced control** — hold to steer one.
- **Control Projectiles** — build up a swarm, then send it. See below.

**`` ` `` + scroll** sets throw distance.

## Control Projectiles

Gather a cloud of projectiles around you, pick a target, launch the lot.

| Input | |
|---|---|
| **Build modifier + right-click** | Summon one into the swarm. One click, one projectile. |
| **Right-click** | Lock a target — mob or block. |
| **Right-click again** | Send everything at it. |
| **Left-click** | Fire one out of the swarm. |
| **Scroll** | Pick the type. |

Every input is a single click. Nothing repeats while you hold a button, and **nothing fires that you
didn't summon** — an empty swarm just tells you so. That's deliberate: the earlier version repeated on a
held button and could conjure projectiles when the swarm ran dry, which meant it could start firing and
not stop.

They spawn facing the way you are and pass straight through you while they float, so they can't bump you
around. Lock onto a mob and it stays locked — it still gets hit if it runs. The staff holds the cast pose
the whole time.

In survival, summoning an **arrow** takes one from your inventory (there's a server setting for it). Only
arrows cost anything — fireballs, TNT and food don't need to be carried.

## Custom projectiles

In the launcher → **✦ Custom Projectiles…**, add any entity by ID, give it a name, and fire it like any
built-in type. They show up in the quick chooser too.
